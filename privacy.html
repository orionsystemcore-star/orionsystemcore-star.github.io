Privacy Policy — Personal Automation

Effective date: November 16, 2026.

What data this app accesses

This app accesses the following Google user data, belonging to the owner only:

  • Google account email address and profile information, used for sign-in.
  • Google Drive files, so the app can read and write the owner's files.
  • Google Sheets, so the app can read and update the owner's spreadsheets.

How the data is used

The data is used only to read and update the owner's own files. It is not used for advertising or analytics.

How the data is stored

Data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own computer. It is not copied to any third-party storage.

Sharing

This app does not share Google user data with any third party, and does not sell Google user data.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in Google account settings under third-party access. To request deletion of any data held by the app, contact orion.system.core@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted on this page.
